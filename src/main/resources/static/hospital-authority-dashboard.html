Hospital Generator Backup Status

Press refresh after pump collection to load updated diesel reserve, backup hours, and status without logout.

Status Backup Hours Meaning Request Mode
CRITICAL Less than 6 hours Hospital cannot survive minimum backup target. Auto approval possible if pump has stock
MIDDLE 6 to less than 8 hours Backup is weak but minimum 6 hours is available. Admin review
RISK FREE 8 to less than 12 hours Hospital has safer backup coverage. Admin review
ENOUGH 12 hours or more Hospital has enough generator diesel reserve. Admin review if request is still submitted
